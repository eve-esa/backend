EVE is open to everyone

You pre-registered for the EVE pilot with person@example.com, so we wanted you to be among the first to know: the Earth Virtual Expert (EVE) is now open to everyone!

Sign in to EVE

How to sign in

  • Pilot account: sign in at https://eve-chat.example.org with the e-mail address and password you used for the pilot.
  • Forgot your password? Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page.

What EVE does

EVE answers Earth Observation questions with responses grounded in EO data and peer-reviewed science, and every answer is traceable to its sources.

What comes next

This is just the beginning. Expect regular updates over the coming weeks and months, including agentic capabilities that will let EVE plan multi-step analyses, retrieve and process EO data, and run tools for you.

Your feedback

We’d love to hear what you think. Send your feedback or questions to eve@picampus-school.com.
